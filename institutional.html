1. Campus Road Infrastructure – Malabar Medical College Hospital Campus

Infrastructure & Civil Work 2019–2025 Healthcare Campus Logistics Makkari Constructions & Developers

A multi-phase civil infrastructure project involving the development, gradient management, and high-durability bituminous paving of internal roads across the expansive Malabar Medical College Hospital campus. Executed across a six-year timeline, the work was carefully planned to ensure seamless campus connectivity while maintaining continuous, uninterrupted access for emergency vehicles and daily hospital traffic.

Execution & Technical Highlights

  • Phased Campus Development: Planned and constructed in stages (2019–2025) to avoid disturbing round-the-clock medical operations, patient transport, and emergency services.
  • Heavy-Duty Asphalt Surfacing: Full-depth earthwork preparation, sub-base compaction, and heavy-duty bituminous laying designed to handle heavy daily traffic from ambulances, supply trucks, and visitor vehicles.
  • Complex Contour & Slope Navigation: Specialized grading and retaining wall alignments designed to manage steep campus elevations and ensure safe vehicle transitions around multi-story hospital blocks.
  • Critical Logistics Flow: Expanded and smooth thoroughfares connecting essential zones—including emergency trauma bays, outpatient departments, parking areas, and service entrances.
