1. The Poonoor Residence

2. The Naduvannur Residence

2,400 Sq. Ft. Karumbapoil, Naduvannur, Kerala Makkari Constructions & Developers Completed 2024

Designed around family traditions and wide open horizons, this home in Naduvannur overlooks lush, green rice fields. Built with the family’s lifestyle at its heart, every space—from its sunlit rooms to its sprawling verandah—was shaped around how they live, relax, and celebrate.

Design Highlights

  • Festival & Paddy Field Views: Designed specifically at the client’s request, a spacious L-shaped verandah and wide balcony frame open views of the rice fields while offering the perfect vantage point to enjoy the annual local temple festival with family and friends.
  • Breeze & Open Space: Positioned to capture cool breezes sweeping across the fields, keeping the home naturally bright and airy throughout the day.
  • Textural Harmony: A rich blend of classic pitched terracotta roof tiles and clean, textured grey stone accents for a timeless modern-tropical aesthetic.
  • Sheltered Outdoor Flow: Deep roof overhangs and shaded porticos provide comfortable, rain-sheltered spots to enjoy the countryside views year-round.

3. The Poonath Residence

Poonath, Kerala Makkari Constructions & Developers 2019–2022

Set deep within Poonath’s lush, forest-like canopy, this residence is a refined blend of regal Arabic geometry and tropical serenity. Designed to make a striking architectural statement without losing the warmth of home, it stands as a quiet, elegant sanctuary surrounded by nature.

Design Highlights

  • Arabic Architectural Elegance: Clean symmetry, stately proportions, and twin hipped rooflines bring a touch of Middle Eastern architectural grandeur to the Kerala landscape.
  • Immersed in Greenery: Tucked gracefully into a dense woodland backdrop, keeping the home naturally shaded, quiet, and cool throughout the hot summer months.
  • Warm, Sophisticated Palette: Crisp off-white facades contrasted with warm timber-toned windows and dark sloped roofs create a timeless, high-end finish.
  • Light & Forest Breezes: Generous window framing invites soft daylight and constant forest breezes deep into the living spaces, blurring the line between inside and out.
