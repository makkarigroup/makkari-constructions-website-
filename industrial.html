1. Industrial Storage & Logistics Hub – Atholi

Atholi, Kozhikode 5-Acre Land | 60,000 Sq. Ft. Facility Design, Consultation & Approvals Makkari Constructions & Developers

Master-planned across a 5-acre campus in Atholi, this 60,000 sq. ft. commercial warehousing hub was engineered for major industrial clients, including Milma and KMCL. Our scope covered total architectural design, spatial planning, and managing all required regulatory documentation and approvals.

Consultation & Planning Highlights

  • End-to-End Compliance & Documentation: Fully managed site planning, architectural drawings, and all statutory paperwork/permits required for a large-scale industrial project.
  • Master-Planned Logistics: Designed broad paved yards and strategic rolling shutter bay placements to ensure seamless heavy-truck movement and simultaneous loading operations.
  • Monsoon-Ready Design: Integrated wide canopy overhangs across loading docks to ensure uninterrupted supply chain operations during heavy rains.

2. Thinner Storage & Logistics Hub – Naduvannur

Naduvannur (Near Kerafed), Kozhikode 24,000 Sq. Ft. + 9,900 Sq. Ft. Facility Design, Technical Consultation & Approvals Makkari Constructions & Developers

A high-specification industrial development engineered for specialized warehousing and supply chain operations. Covering a 24,000 sq. ft. Category-I Thinner Storage Building and a 9,900 sq. ft. EFF Logistics Hub, our scope encompassed structural layout design, industrial site master planning, and total management of all complex regulatory approvals.

Consultation & Planning Highlights

  • Specialized Regulatory Compliance: Managed end-to-end statutory paperwork, technical clearances, and safety documentation required for Category-I flammable chemical storage.
  • Pre-Engineered Steel Architecture: Designed durable, high-clearance pre-engineered building (PEB) structures with expansive clear spans, high ventilation standards, and heavy-duty industrial framing.
  • Efficient Industrial Layout: Master-planned to ensure safe material handling, hazard isolation, and smooth heavy-vehicle transport access near the Kerafed industrial zone in Naduvannur.

3. Bharat Petroleum (BPCL) Retail Outlet

Naduvannur, Kerala Fuel Retail Infrastructure Layout Redesign, Approvals & Supervision Makkari Constructions & Developers

When the initial master plan provided by the oil company was rejected due to site constraints, our team stepped in to completely redraw and re-engineer the entire layout from scratch. By aligning the design with site topography, road safety norms, and local regulatory requirements, we successfully navigated the approval process and led the project through execution.

Scope & Problem-Solving Highlights

  • Custom Master Plan Redesign: Re-engineered and completely redrew the site layout from the ground up—optimizing traffic circulation, driveways, canopy positioning, and underground tank layouts to meet stringent safety distance guidelines.
  • End-to-End Statutory Approvals: Managed all complex paperwork and secured necessary regulatory clearances, including PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization), District Magistrate NOC, Fire & Rescue Services, Highway/PWD permissions, and Pollution Control Board compliance.
  • On-Site Construction Supervision: Provided hands-on technical supervision during civil, structural, and canopy installation phases to ensure exact execution according to the newly approved design.
