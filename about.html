About Us

Founded in 2019, Makkari Constructions and Developers has grown into one of the trusted names in the construction and development sector in Kerala. With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we specialize in delivering both government and private projects that stand as a symbol of reliability and excellence.

At Makkari Constructions, we believe every structure tells a story — of vision, precision, and dedication. Our team of skilled engineers, licensed contractors, and professionals work together to transform ideas into enduring realities. From residential buildings and commercial spaces to institutional and hospital projects, we ensure every project meets the highest standards of safety and aesthetics.

Our Leadership & Team

My journey in construction began after completing my Bachelor of Engineering in Civil Engineering. From the beginning, my vision was not just to complete projects, but to deliver developments that create lasting value for people and communities.

I first worked with ICICI Bank, where I was introduced to the financial and technical aspects of construction projects. Soon after, I moved to Qatar to join Hamton International as an Assistant Project Engineer. There, I gained hands-on experience from the ground up, managing major works including a 100-villa residential development and the Al Andalus School project. These responsibilities shaped my ability to handle large-scale projects with precision and accountability.

On returning to India, I joined Malabar Medical College as a Project Engineer, handling significant institutional developments. Since then, they have continued with me as a valued client, reflecting the strong, trust-based partnerships that guide my career.

A major turning point came when I obtained my PWD Contractor License, establishing Makkari Constructions and Developers with a focus on premium residential, commercial, and institutional projects.

Over time, I strengthened my professional standing by becoming a Chartered Engineer (C.Eng, IEI) and completing my Master of Engineering in Structural Engineering, qualifications that reinforced both my technical expertise and leadership in the field.

Today, through Makkari Constructions and Developers, I remain committed to one vision: to create sustainable, innovative, and trusted developments that stand the test of time.
